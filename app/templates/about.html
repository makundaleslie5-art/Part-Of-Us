About pART of US

Community, Health and Art — together we grow.

WHO WE ARE

Everyone deserves to be part of something meaningful.

pART Of US is a Community Driven Organisation committed to promoting mental health awareness, empowering young people and supporting vulnerable communities.

We work towards a society where mental health is understood, stigma is challenged, opportunities are created and no one feels forgotten.

OUR BELIEF

Mental health is not a privilege. It is part of being human.

A community grows stronger when its people feel supported, valued and empowered.

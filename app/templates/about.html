GET TO KNOW US

About pART of US

Community, Health and Art — together we grow.

WHO WE ARE

A community where every person belongs.

Part of Us is a community-driven mental health initiative dedicated to creating safe, inclusive spaces for healing and growth. We support men and women, young people, older persons, people living with disabilities and caregivers.

By reducing stigma, improving access to mental health education and encouraging supportive connections, we bring people together to share stories, find strength and foster resilience.

OUR STORY

From a part of me to a part of us.

Part of Us grew from recognising the silent struggles and unspoken emotions that many people carry. We wanted to create a safe space where people could have honest conversations about life, purpose, hope and the experiences that shape us.

What began as an idea centred on creative expression, poetry and spoken word evolved into something bigger. The name became Part of Us because these conversations, experiences and hopes belong to everyone.

Our journey is guided by two connected ideas: community and artistry, where creativity becomes a language for the soul; and community and health, where mental well-being, awareness and supportive relationships help people grow.

OUR MISSION

What we exist to do

To nurture a community where every person—of every age, gender, and ability—can thrive in mind and spirit by breaking stigma, opening doors to care and knowledge, and using the unifying power of art to heal, empower and inspire hope.

OUR VISION

The future we are working towards

A community where every person is free from stigma and supported to flourish in mind, body, and spirit through inclusion, care, and the power of art.

OUR VALUES

The principles that guide us.

These values shape how we listen, connect, support and grow together.

01

Truth & Authenticity

We encourage people to show up as they truly are and create room for honest expression without pressure to conform.

02

Inclusive Connection

Everyone belongs. We embrace diversity, value every voice and approach others with compassion.

03

Growth & Healing

We recognise that growth is not always linear and healing is not always visible. We value reflection and self-awareness.

04

Integrity & Credibility

We are committed to carrying out our work responsibly, truthfully and reliably.

05

Safe & Confidential Spaces

We value spaces where people can feel respected, heard and safe when sharing their experiences.

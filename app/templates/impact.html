OUR ACTION PLAN

Turning our vision into action.

These are our planned targets as we grow. We will use activities, feedback and documented outcomes to track our progress.

01

Consistent Content Creation

Share storytelling, mental health awareness, spoken word and community reflections.

Target: 3–4 posts per week across platforms.

02

Speaking & Engagement

Participate in school talks, community events, workshops and mental health forums.

Target: At least 6 speaking engagements or school sessions in the first year.

03

Community Events

Create safe spaces for mental health discussions, creative expression and shared experiences.

Target: At least 4 events annually, with 30+ attendees per event.

04

Digital Development

Build our website as a central hub for resources, stories, content and community work.

Target: Website launch within six months.

05

Mental Health Support App

Develop a concept for accessible digital tools, reflection and safe community engagement.

Target: Complete the concept and wireframe within 12 months and seek a development partner or grant.

HOW WE MEASURE PROGRESS

Impact is more than numbers.

We intend to monitor audience feedback, event attendance, participant experiences, testimonials, content engagement and the partnerships we build.

Our goal is to learn from the communities we serve and use that learning to improve our programmes and strengthen our work.

OUR COMMITMENT

Meaningful change begins when we stand together.

Ultimately, the most meaningful result is that someone, somewhere, feels less alone because of pART of US.

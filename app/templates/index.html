WHO WE ARE

Everyone deserves to be part of something meaningful.

pART Of US is a Community Driven Organisation committed to promoting mental health awareness, empowering young people and supporting vulnerable communities.

We work towards a society where mental health is understood, stigma is challenged, opportunities are created and no one feels forgotten.

OUR AREAS OF FOCUS

Creating change, one community at a time.

Our programmes address the interconnected challenges affecting individuals, families and communities.

01

Mental Health Awareness

Promoting mental well-being, emotional awareness, stigma reduction and access to appropriate support.

02

Youth Empowerment

Inspiring young people through mentorship, leadership, life skills and personal development.

03

Education & Opportunities

Supporting education, knowledge sharing and opportunities for lifelong learning.

04

Substance Abuse Prevention

Encouraging informed choices through prevention education, awareness and support referrals.

05

Suicide Prevention

Encouraging open conversations, early help-seeking and connections to appropriate professional care.

06

Community Empowerment

Addressing poverty, vulnerability and social challenges through sustainable community initiatives.

07

Gender-Based Violence Prevention

Promoting dignity, safety, awareness and survivor-centred support and referrals.

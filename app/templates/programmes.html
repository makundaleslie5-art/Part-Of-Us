OUR AREAS OF FOCUS

Our Programmes

Creating change, one community at a time.

WHAT WE CARE ABOUT

Building stronger, healthier and more empowered communities.

Our areas of focus address interconnected challenges affecting individuals, families and communities.

01

Mental Health Awareness

Promoting mental well-being, emotional awareness, stigma reduction and access to appropriate information and support.

02

Youth Empowerment

Inspiring young people through mentorship, leadership, life skills, identity development and personal growth.

03

Education & Opportunities

Supporting education, knowledge sharing and opportunities for lifelong learning.

04

Substance Abuse Prevention

Encouraging informed choices through prevention education, awareness and connections to appropriate support.

05

Suicide Prevention

Encouraging open conversations, early help-seeking and connections to appropriate professional care.

06

Community Empowerment

Building supportive communities through inclusion, shared learning and community-led initiatives.

07

Gender-Based Violence Prevention

Promoting dignity, safety, awareness and access to appropriate survivor-centred support.

08

Creative Expression & Art

Using poetry, spoken word, storytelling and other creative forms to encourage expression, connection and hope.

OUR COMMUNITY

Who we seek to support.

Our work recognises that people experience different challenges and need inclusive spaces where they can be heard.

Youth & Teenagers

Creating opportunities for safe expression, identity development, emotional awareness and conversations about peer pressure.

People Living with Disabilities

Encouraging inclusion, accessibility, dignity and supportive community connections.

The Elderly Community

Promoting connection through reflection, storytelling and intergenerational relationships while addressing isolation.

Caregivers

Recognising caregivers and encouraging attention to their own emotional well-being and support needs.

OUR ANNUAL AWARENESS CALENDAR

MENTAL HEALTH ROUND THE YEAR DATES

A year-round guide to mental health awareness, education, conversations and opportunities to connect.

These dates are drawn from the Part Of Us branding workbook and include global and international observances. They are not all officially designated Kenyan national observances. Some dates and awareness weeks may change from year to year.

JANUARY

Mental Wellness Month

All month

An invitation to focus on intentional well-being, self-care and sustainable habits that support mental wellness.

FEBRUARY

National Eating Disorders Awareness Week

February 23 – March 1

Promoting early recognition, access to treatment and support, while challenging myths and stigma surrounding eating disorders.

MARCH

Self-Injury Awareness Day

March 1

Encouraging open, compassionate conversations, reducing shame and helping people connect with appropriate counselling or crisis support.

National Sleep Awareness Week

March 8–14

Highlighting the relationship between sleep, emotional well-being and mental health.

World Sleep Day

March 13

A global call to action on sleep health and sleep disorders, led by the World Sleep Society.

World Bipolar Day

March 30

Increasing understanding of bipolar disorder and challenging stigma.

APRIL

Stress Awareness Month

All month

Encouraging people to identify stressors, understand their effects and explore healthy coping strategies.

Alcohol Awareness Month

All month

Raising awareness of the relationship between alcohol use and mental health, and pathways to support and recovery.

MAY

Mental Health Awareness Month

May 1–31

Creating opportunities to learn, speak openly about mental health and encourage supportive communities.

Maternal Mental Health Awareness Week

May 4–10

Highlighting perinatal mental health and the importance of support during pregnancy and after childbirth.

World Maternal Mental Health Day

First Wednesday in May

Raising awareness of maternal mental health and the need for understanding and appropriate care.

National Children's Mental Health Awareness Day

May 7

Drawing attention to children's mental health and the importance of early understanding and support.

JUNE

Men's Mental Health Awareness Month

All month

Encouraging men and boys to speak about emotional well-being, seek support and challenge harmful stigma.

PTSD Awareness Month

All month

Increasing understanding of post-traumatic stress disorder and the importance of appropriate support.

JULY

Minority Mental Health Awareness Month

All month

Also known as Bebe Moore Campbell National Minority Mental Health Awareness Month. It highlights mental health experiences and barriers to care among underserved communities.

AUGUST

National Grief Awareness Day

August 30

Creating space for conversations about grief, loss, compassion and the different ways people experience bereavement.

SEPTEMBER

National Recovery Month

All month

Recognising recovery, reducing stigma and promoting awareness of support for mental health and substance use challenges.

Suicide Prevention Week

September 6–12

Encouraging awareness, compassionate conversations and connection to appropriate support.

World Suicide Prevention Day

September 10

A global observance associated with the World Health Organization and International Association for Suicide Prevention.

OCTOBER

National Day of Prayer for Mental Illness Recovery

October 6

An opportunity for reflection, compassion and support for people affected by mental health challenges.

National Depression Screening Day

October 8

Promoting awareness of depression and encouraging people to seek appropriate screening and professional guidance.

World Mental Health Day

October 10

A global day to raise awareness, encourage action and promote mental health for all. It is also marked by institutions and mental health stakeholders in Kenya.

OCD Awareness Week

October 12–18

Increasing understanding of obsessive-compulsive disorder and challenging misconceptions.

Global Peer Support Celebration Day

October 15

Recognising the value of peer support, shared experience and connection.

NOVEMBER

Movember

All month

Encouraging conversations about men's mental health, well-being and suicide prevention.

International Men's Day

November 19

Recognising men's contributions and encouraging positive conversations about men's health and well-being.

International Survivors of Suicide Loss Day

November 21

Providing space to acknowledge and support people who have lost someone to suicide.

DECEMBER

International Day of Persons with Disabilities

December 3

Promoting the rights, dignity, inclusion and well-being of persons with disabilities.

Planning note: Awareness weeks, movable observances and annual themes may vary. Part Of Us should confirm the relevant year's dates and themes with the respective organisers before publishing event announcements.
